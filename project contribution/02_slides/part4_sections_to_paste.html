Part 4 · Video nutrition label 22

How it would work

three moments,
one clock

The label informs, it doesn't block. The user sets the limit and decides what happens next.

01 · while scrolling
a clock in the casino
76 videos today · mostly Local news (39%)

A small counter stays on screen. Nothing is blocked: the clock is simply back.

02 · at your limit
a pause, not a wall
You reached your limit. Today's mix:
Stop here  ·  10 more

At a limit the user chose, the feed pauses and shows what the sitting was made of. One tap continues.

03 · next morning
the daily label
Yesterday, bottled
76 videos · replays 48% · "about 39% of your time went to Local news…"

The full label with the AI summary, once a day, when the user is not in the feed.

A third party (plug-in, independent app) can

Build moment 03 from the user's own data download (GDPR Art. 15 and 20) · run moment 01 on the web version via a browser plug-in · tag videos by topic with an AI model

Only the platform can

Show moments 01 and 02 inside the mobile app · see why a video was recommended · change what the feed optimises
