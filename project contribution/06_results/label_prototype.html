what is your scrolling made of?

Food comes with a label. Short video doesn't. This prototype turns real Kuaishou viewing logs into a one-glance label: how much, what kind, how much of it is replays and late-night viewing, and a short summary written by an AI model.

Your viewing, bottled (share of watch time)
AI summary
How the summary is made
  • The model sees only the numbers on the label, not your watch history.
  • It must use only those numbers, stay neutral and give one optional suggestion.
  • No diagnosis, no moralising. The label informs, you decide.

three moments

A label only helps if it shows up at the right time. These mock-ups use the selected user's numbers.

01 · while scrolling

a clock in the casino

A small, always-visible counter. It doesn't block anything: it just puts the clock back.

02 · at your limit

a pause, not a wall

The user sets the limit. When it's reached, the feed pauses and shows what the sitting was made of. One tap continues.

03 · next morning

the daily label

The full label and the AI summary, once a day, when the user isn't in the feed.

who can build this?

The platform could build all three moments tomorrow: it already has every number. An outside app (a "third party") can only do part of it.

The platform (TikTok, Kuaishou)A third party (browser plug-in, independent app)
01 · while scrollingYes, it owns the screenOnly on the web version, via a browser plug-in. Inside the mobile apps: no, outside apps can't read another app's screen.
02 · at your limitYesPartly: phone settings (Screen Time, Digital Wellbeing) can limit total app time, but can't tell what the time was made of.
03 · next morningYesYes, but delayed: the user can download their own watch history (GDPR right of access, Art. 15, and data portability, Art. 20). The export has no categories, so an AI model would have to tag the videos from their titles.
Change the feed itselfYesNo. A third party can inform the user, not change what the recommender optimises.
